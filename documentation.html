Getting Started

Introduction to GSM Pay

GSM Pay is the modern payment layer for digital businesses — accept USDT, USDC, and Binance Pay directly from your storefront or invoice, with funds settling in your merchant wallet within seconds. This guide walks you through every feature in the merchant dashboard, from your first login to your first payout.

Getting Started

Dashboard Overview

Everything you need lives in one place. The left sidebar gives you quick access to transactions, payouts, and settings, while the main panel surfaces your live balance, recent activity, and settlement status at a glance.

Payment Methods

Accepting USDT

USDT (TRC20 & ERC20) is enabled by default on every GSM Pay account. Customers can pay from any compatible wallet — funds are credited to your balance once the network confirms the transaction, typically within seconds on TRC20.

Steps

01
Open Payment Methods

Go to Settings → Payment Methods in your dashboard.

02
Toggle USDT on

Select your preferred network (TRC20 or ERC20) and save.

03
Start accepting payments

USDT now appears as an option on your checkout page.

Payment Methods

Accepting USDC

USDC works the same way as USDT — enable it once from your payment settings and it becomes available to every customer at checkout. USDC settlements are fully on-chain and verified automatically by GSM Pay before your balance updates.

Payment Methods

Enabling Binance Pay (C2C)

Binance Pay lets your customers settle directly from their Binance account using the built-in C2C transfer flow — no external wallet app required. This is one of the fastest checkout experiences available on GSM Pay.

Steps

01
Connect your Binance account

Go to Settings → Binance Pay and authorize the connection.

02
Verify ownership

Confirm the one-time code sent to your Binance app.

03
Enable at checkout

Turn on the Binance Pay (C2C) toggle and save your changes.

04
Done

Binance Pay now appears as a checkout option for every customer.

Account & Settings

Withdrawing Funds

Once your balance settles, you can withdraw to any external wallet address at any time. Withdrawals are processed within minutes and a confirmation is sent to your registered email.

Account & Settings

Two-Factor Authentication

We strongly recommend enabling 2FA on every merchant account. Once active, you'll be asked for a one-time code from your authenticator app whenever you log in or initiate a withdrawal.

Save your recovery codes. If you lose access to your authenticator app, recovery codes are the only way to regain access to your account.

Troubleshooting

Failed Transactions

A transaction can fail for a few common reasons — insufficient network confirmations, the customer cancelling mid-checkout, or an expired session. Failed transactions never deduct funds from the customer and can always be safely retried.
